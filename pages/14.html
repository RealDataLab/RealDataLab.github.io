The Bathymetry Map project offers an interactive exploration of the underwater topography of oceans, seas, and deep lakes across varying geographical scales.

Bathymetry is the study of underwater depth, effectively serving as the underwater equivalent to hypsometry or topography. Understanding these submerged landscapes is crucial for maritime navigation, geographic research, and environmental and coastal management.

The visualization in this application reveals the complex terrain beneath the water's surface, including deep ocean trenches, mid-ocean ridges, and coastal continental shelves—providing a unique spatial perspective on the hidden features of our planet.
